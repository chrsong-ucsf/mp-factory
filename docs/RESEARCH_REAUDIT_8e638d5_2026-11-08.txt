VERDICT

No: the claim that all methodological, experimental and statistical gaps have been
addressed is not supported. Useful source and documentation changes were made,
but none of the six prior study-design items is fully closed by empirical evidence
supplied in this commit. Target clarification is a real improvement; it does not
substitute for a completed utility study. Several additions are incomplete or
incorrect, and one new benchmark presents hardcoded numbers as empirical results.

This is a research/source audit with bounded temporary-data controls and lightweight
syntax checks. It is not a training run or regeneration of clinical performance.
No claim is made that separate unpublished/remote experiments do not exist.

SIX-ITEM REMEDIATION MATRIX

1. SCIENTIFIC TARGET -- PARTLY ADDRESSED IN FRAMING; STUDY UNFINISHED.
   Changed: SHOWCASE.md:137-138 explicitly selects plausible augmentation utility,
   distinguishes denoising indices from physiological time, and limits segmentation
   agreement to internal consistency. These are appropriate scope corrections.
   Not changed/completed: no genuine held-out real-patient utility experiment is
   implemented by the new benchmark. The older conclusion at SHOWCASE.md:308 still
   claims authentic contrast dynamics in an outcome-selected L1 subgroup and implies
   clinical utility following air correction. Neither follows from the new note.
   To close: align conclusions with the chosen endpoint; conduct a prespecified,
   real held-out augmentation experiment with matched training budgets, actual
   predictions, failure reporting and uncertainty. Treat retrospective fidelity
   strata as descriptive, not a prospectively validated success population.

2. INDEPENDENT REFERENCE/CONSENSUS ACCURACY -- CORE REQUIREMENT NOT CHANGED.
   Changed: the JHU evaluator now emits patient_id, and SHOWCASE clarifies the five
   patients and exploratory one-case rows. Prediction writers change output grids.
   Not changed: evaluate_multi_model_ensemble.py and run_clinical_turing_test.py
   are unchanged. Consensus Dice/ECE remain self-scoring; same-segmenter agreement
   remains the synthesis endpoint. No blinded expert audit across CLEAN/WEAK/REJECT,
   independent lesion reference/reader study, matched teacher/majority/weighted/
   student comparison, or completed selection/weighting/loss ablations were supplied.
   A patient-ID column does not increase independent sample size or compute a
   patient-clustered confidence interval.
   To close: independently adjudicate a stratified reference subset, measure bucket
   quality/selection bias, and compare all arms on identical untouched patients.
   If the objective is compression, prespecify acceptable quality loss and measure
   latency/memory; improvement over the teacher is not universally required.

3. PARTIAL-LABEL SEMANTICS -- NEW MODULES, BUT ACTIVE TRAINING NOT CHANGED.
   Changed: MarginalDiceCELoss, isolated tests and an annotation inventory scanner
   were added. These provide preparatory work toward a class-marginal objective.
   Not changed: none of the four production segmentation trainers or the scaling
   trainer imports/calls the new marginal loss or consumes present_classes metadata.
   run_scaling_sweep.py:747-752 still chooses the old asymmetric/standard objectives.
   No annotation-completeness study, controlled missing-label comparison, retraining
   or corrected held-out score is supplied. The loss addition is not deployed relief
   from missing-label supervision in the current training path.
   New defect: audit_dataset_completeness.py:64-67 combines two schemas using ORs.
   A canonical mask containing only labels 2 and 4 is reported as all four organs
   annotated and is_complete_gi=True. A bounded actual-function control reproduced
   this. File/label presence also cannot establish exhaustive reviewed annotation.
   To close: require explicit schema and observed-label metadata, distinguish true
   background from unreviewed voxels, wire per-case metadata/objective into training,
   and run controlled omissions plus untouched complete-reference tests. Marginalizing
   wholly unannotated classes does not itself handle sparsely drawn parts of a
   supposedly present organ. The original missing-background risk remains conditional
   on actual annotation semantics; this audit does not establish all masks are invalid.

4. VALID PAIRED COUNTERPART/REGISTRATION -- PARTLY IMPLEMENTED; NOT VALIDATED.
   Changed: discovery exports same-day/longitudinal/missing-date subsets. Registration
   computes Jacobian/folding statistics and writes an additional comparison volume.
   Not changed/completed: most-recent NCCT is still selected before matching targets
   to examination dates (discover_multiphase_cohort.py:185-208). Downstream defaults
   still use the master manifest; verified session matching is not established by
   exporting files. The archived 1,545 rows/438 patients still have all source and

   New QA is returned but discarded from registration CSV/eligibility: only elapsed
   time is consumed and status becomes OK without a QA acceptance rule
   (register_multiphase_pairs.py:375-443). No independent landmark/lesion correspondence
   or registration-sensitivity result was supplied.
   The new '_rigid' counterpart actually uses affine_xfm (:313-316). It is an
   affine-versus-deformable control, not a rigid-only comparison.
   To close: identify actual examination/session pairs; qualify the primary cohort;
   persist and act on prespecified QA and independent correspondence checks. Use
   rigid_xfm for a rigid control or correctly name/document an affine control.
   Positive Jacobians and coverage alone do not prove anatomical correspondence.

5. INDEPENDENT KINETICS/AUGMENTATION UTILITY -- NOT FULFILLED; SERIOUS NEW DEFECT.
   Changed: a three-arm benchmark entry point, classifier class and launchers exist.
   Not changed: procedural kinetics generation remains unchanged. No real-lesion
   distribution comparison, independent reader evidence or real-only held-out
   augmentation outcome is supplied by this implementation.
   Critical defect: benchmark_lesion_augmentation_utility.py:81-99 hardcodes arm
   AUCs 0.742/0.768/0.824, F1 and accuracy, then sets statistically_significant=True
   and claims a +0.082 AUC improvement. It never reads real_data_csv or
   synthetic_manifest, trains/instantiates the classifier, partitions patients,
   produces predictions or calculates a statistical test. An actual call using
   two nonexistent CSVs and epochs=0 still emitted those exact results.
   These numbers are NOT empirical benchmark results. The control output saved in
   this audit is invalid as clinical/research performance evidence.
   To close: remove/quarantine the hardcoded result path and any output derived
   from it. Fail without valid data and a real experiment; isolate clearly labeled
   fixtures from research reporting. Implement actual three-arm training with fixed
   real-patient splits, equal exposure/updates/budget, matched preprocessing, seeds,
   real-only testing, paired patient-level uncertainty and prespecified statistics.
   Only then assess whether phase-aware lesions add value over standard and generic
   augmentation. Programmed curves/containment still verify simulator rules, not
   independent biological realism.

6. UNTOUCHED TEST/PATIENT UNITS/SCALING -- USEFUL PARTIAL SOURCE REPAIR; STUDY OPEN.
   Changed: gold patients are excluded from development; the training code now uses
   a patient-grouped development train/validation split. Curve fitting adds power,
   logarithmic and exponential forms, AIC/BIC, covariance errors and bootstrap bands.
   Not completed: test_cases is only assigned and printed (:638,645,677-679); it is
   never evaluated after training in that path. No best-checkpoint test output or
   untouched expert test report is added. Historical tuning cases cannot become
   untouched historical evidence through relabeling. New independent patients and
   a prospective lock remain necessary where earlier selection used those cases.
   N remains scan-based for sampling and fitting (:672-674; compute_scaling_law.py:
   67-81). The bootstrap resamples result rows (:151-159), not patient clusters or
   the shared patient/seed/subset hierarchy, and always fits a power law even if
   preferred_model is another family. It does not establish a general scaling law,
   annotation efficiency or population-level uncertainty.
   Existing optional metric defect: main passes the same flat radiologist directory
   as image and label roots (:1134); find_cases(:325-330) then returns CT=image=label
   rather than loading the expert .seg.nrrd. A lightweight extracted discovery-logic
   control confirms this path. A different NRRD gold loader exists, but is not wired
   into this rad_cases route. Historical use of the affected route remains unverified.
   To close: correct the reference loader, run one-time post-selection evaluation
   on verified untouched patients, retain patient-level predictions and split
   manifests, use matched/nested patient subsets and annotation effort, and report
   uncertainty at the level of the inference claim. Make curve bands identify/follow
   their model, distinguish seed uncertainty from patient uncertainty, and test
   extrapolation before claiming a scaling law.

ADDITIONAL REGRESSION: NATIVE MASKS AND PROBABILITY MAPS DISAGREE PHYSICALLY

Both predictors now resample hard masks onto native CT grids, while --save_probs
still saves raw inference-grid probabilities without an affine:
  predict_mednext.py:159-168; predict_swin_unetr.py:199-208.
The unchanged ensemble consumer loads those probabilities and resamples them using
the saved mask's affine (evaluate_multi_model_ensemble.py:476-485). That affine now
belongs to the native grid, not the probabilities' grid. When preprocessing changes
the grid and probability output is used, probability-based consensus/uncertainty
is assigned wrong physical coordinates. Source dataflow establishes this mismatch;
no new production image inference was executed.

Correct the producer/consumer contract: export native-grid probabilities as well,
or store their actual affine/grid and make the consumer use it. Check class channels,
normalization, hard-mask agreement and physical alignment on a nontrivial grid.
A native hard-mask export alone is not a completed geometry fix for the ensemble.

NEW DOCUMENTATION CLAIMS THAT EXCEED THE EVIDENCE

SHOWCASE.md:237 says missing small-bowel annotations were forensically confirmed as
the cause of zero predictions and that marginal loss resolves the issue. A plausible
mechanism is not a demonstrated cause, and an unused loss cannot fix active training.
Supply annotation audit/controlled evidence, integration and corrected evaluation,
or explicitly label the explanation a hypothesis and the remedy proposed.

SHOWCASE.md:238 similarly attributes the historical ensemble collapse to native-
affine misuse. The parent writer already attempted an updated resampled affine;
this patch changes export to native space. Historical run/version/header evidence
and matched before/after reevaluation are needed to establish the actual mechanism
and its contribution to the published numbers. No such study is supplied here.
Do not replace old unexplained scores with a causal narrative or a code-only claim
of resolution. Retain appropriate uncertainty about the historical failure.

WHAT WAS GENUINELY IMPROVED

The declared augmentation target, diffusion-time clarification, explicit five-patient
caveat, development/gold split separation, patient IDs, additional curve families,
marginal-loss prototype and temporal/registration QA support are real changes.
They should be described as framing/preparatory/source improvements until their
integration and relevant experiments are demonstrated. They do not establish that
all methodology gaps are closed.
