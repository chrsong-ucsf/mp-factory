Project 10 theoretical/methods audit at d2931d4, November 8
Scope: current source/docs and primary research; no training, volume verification, cluster access, or tests. These are research-design limitations, not claims that NCCT-based synthesis is impossible or that clinical harm has occurred.

R1 [Research blocker] The estimand is underspecified: plausible conditional contrast appearance versus this patient's true enhancement.
Evidence: docs/SHOWCASE.md:297 calls the outcome-selected top30.1% L1 subgroup 'authentic contrast dynamics'. code/project10/models/synthesis_net.py:62-87 accepts NCCT latent, anatomy, diffusion step and categorical phase; no actual acquisition timestamp or injection protocol is represented. Diffusion time indexes denoising, not physiological time. code/project10/evaluation/sample_multiphase_diffusion.py:105-106 initializes random noise and :719-726 generates a phase-conditioned realization; eta0 removes further trajectory noise but does not remove randomness of the initial latent. evaluate_synthesis.py:130-138,167-207 compares the generated realization against one observed registered target using global image metrics.
Inference: this can support learning a conditional image distribution and exploring useful visualization/augmentation. It does not establish that one generated realization recovers an individual patient's unobserved contrast response. Categorical phase selection does not establish continuous4D kinetics or generalization to an unseen phase. The favorable L1-selected subgroup is selected using the outcome being claimed; it cannot establish prospective performance on an identifiable pre-generation population.
Acceptance: predefine a primary scientific goal (incremental task benefit or patient-specific enhancement accuracy). Freeze a patient/center-held-out test cohort and report all cases, phase strata and failures. Compare identity-NCCT, simple phase-wise intensity mapping, deterministic regression, and the diffusion model with matched preprocessing. Ablate anatomy conditioning and phase conditioning. Include lesion/vessel ROI enhancement, lesion-to-host contrast and independent lesion-preservation/error endpoints rather than only global similarity. If distribution modeling is claimed, evaluate repeated samples and calibration/proper scoring against held-out observations without best-of-K target selection; sampling spread alone is not validated physiological uncertainty. If continuous-time dynamics are claimed, acquire actual timestamps and evaluate held-out time points; an untrained categorical embedding is not an interpolation experiment.
Primary-source context: Pinnock et al., Phys Med Biol2024, explicitly investigates time conditioning and receptive-field ablations for contrast synthesis, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11103281/ (doi10.1088/1361-6560/ad46dd). This supports treating those as testable research variables; it does not prove a universal impossibility result.

R2 [Research blocker] The pairing/registration target can confound contrast synthesis with anatomy changes and registration artifacts; current downstream agreement is not independent anatomical validation.
Evidence: discover_multiphase_cohort.py:185-194 selects a most-recent NCCT and all non-NCCT scans for a patient; :196-208 distinguishes missing dates/longitudinal pairs and makes same-day filtering optional. register_multiphase_pairs.py:284-301 performs rigid+affine+B-spline registration and evaluates the resampled target. The target is therefore a transformed measurement, not a directly observed voxel-aligned counterfactual. run_clinical_turing_test.py:126-152 compares the same segmenter's real/synthetic predictions, excludes organs undetected in its real prediction and allows a pass with one eligible organ at meanDice>=.70. docs/SHOWCASE.md:137 describes this frozen-model audit.
Inference: physical support validation addresses which voxels were acquired, not whether the correspondence is anatomically correct. Patient matching alone does not isolate contrast as the only varying cause; deformation can alter lesion geometry and interpolation can change image statistics. Agreement of one segmenter under two inputs can reflect shared errors and its detection/eligibility selection, so it cannot independently establish lesion preservation or clinical interchangeability. This does not imply any particular current registration is bad; correspondence quality has not been established by those endpoints.
Acceptance: restrict the primary paired test to verified same-examination/same-session acquisitions with protocol/timing data. Treat longitudinal/missing-date pairs as a separately reported sensitivity cohort. Prespecify registration QA using held-out landmarks, lesion boundary review and deformation/Jacobian checks; report exclusions and sensitivity to rigid-only versus deformable targets. Score clinically relevant ROIs and geometry in native coordinates when feasible. Use blinded independent readers or independent reference annotations, include lesions absent/uncertain on NCCT, and count undetected/unscorable structures explicitly. Compare NCCT alone, NCCT+synthetic, and real contrast on a fixed task with patient-level uncertainty. Registration/reader uncertainty should be assessed separately from generator uncertainty.
Primary-source context: Choi et al., Sci Rep2021, separates technical similarity testing from a lesionCNR/observer-conspicuity study, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34650076/ (doi10.1038/s41598-021-00058-3). Pinnock2024 also treats inter-series alignment as a model-design challenge. These are examples of independent endpoints, not transferable performance claims for this project.

R3 [Research blocker for physiological/diagnostic claims] Procedural kinetics validation is circular with respect to the rules used to create the lesions.
Evidence: multiphase_tumor_kinetics.py:115-151 hardcodes tumor-type/phase HU offsets; :282-295 samples template texture and adds the specified core/ring deltas; :297-305 restores every outside-lesion voxel exactly. verify_production_results.py:595-605 tests only that a toyHCC lesion changes inside and has zero change outside. Consequently archetypal curves and zero bleed are construction properties. docs/SHOWCASE.md:159-174 already acknowledges offset/ring templates rather than independent pharmacokinetic modeling, but still labels the section4Dperfusion, invokes neovascularization (:167) and summarizes procedural hemodynamics (:298).
Inference: generating the desired HU pattern and checking the desired pattern is useful simulator/software verification. It does not validate vascular physiology, pathological diversity or improvement on real disease. A diagnostic model trained on strongly type-specific templates may learn a simulator shortcut; this is a research risk requiring testing, not a measured failure here. Discrete HU offsets/rings contain no estimated patient-specific perfusion parameters or continuous contrast transport model.
Acceptance: describe the present method as phase-conditioned procedural lesion augmentation. Validate against a held-out real multiphase lesion cohort with independent subtype/reference labels, lesion/host-relative enhancement distributions, temporal variability and atypical cases; do not use the same template thresholds as outcome truth. Train otherwise-identical downstream models with real-only data, generic lesion augmentation, geometry-only augmentation and phase-template augmentation; test solely on untouched real patients/centers. Measure detection/characterization benefit and calibration, and audit shortcut sensitivity (template swaps, atypical kinetics, lesion-size/texture controls). A4Dphysiology claim additionally requires a specified temporal model and independent time-resolved validation.

Priority: define R1's estimand first; establish R2's valid paired/independent evaluation; then test R3's augmentation benefit. Code execution integrity and prospective scientific validity are distinct evidence requirements. No new model complexity is justified before these acceptance studies.
