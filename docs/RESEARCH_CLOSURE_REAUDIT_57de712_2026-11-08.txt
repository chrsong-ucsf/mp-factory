R03 [P1; F12] Foreground positivity is incorrectly used as spatial coverage.
Source: code/evaluation/evaluate_multi_model_ensemble.py:505,528-558.
When probabilities/support are unavailable, support=(hard_mask>0); NPZ support fallback similarly uses foreground probability. Legitimate background predictions therefore lose their votes. Bounded counterexample: equal-weight full-coverage BG,BG,organ becomes support0,0,1 and unanimous organ instead of majority background. This is an actual common hard-mask/TotalSeg path, not merely an alternate metadata permutation.
Close by deriving spatial coverage from image/grid validity or an explicit validated support map. Never infer coverage from predicted foreground. Test background-majority, supported-negative and partial-field-of-view cases through the production ensemble path.
