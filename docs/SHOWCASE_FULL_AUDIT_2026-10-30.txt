IMPLEMENTATION FINDINGS

I1 [P1] Fresh sampling fails before input selection, then reports completion.
   sample_multiphase_diffusion.py:312-315 references undefined REPO_ROOT;
   the module defines BASE_DIR. Executing the exact input-resolution block
   raises NameError even when candidate NCCT paths were supplied. The broad
   handler at364-367 logs individual errors and still announces completion.
   Fix the name, exercise a full small inference path, and propagate failed
   sample counts/nonzero status. Existing outputs skipped at294-296 also need
   run provenance before being accepted as regenerated results.

I2 [P1] JHU evaluator ignores physical displacement in its fast path.
   evaluate_jhu_radiologist_gpu.py:315-316 accepts equal size/direction without
   checking origin or spacing. A fresh actual-evaluator test on temporary
   5x5x5 masks with prediction origin shifted1000mm gave stomach Dice1.0;
   identity-transform physical resampling gives0.0. Use all grid metadata,
   or always resample onto the reference physical grid.

I3 [P1] Other valid prediction geometry is overwritten.
   evaluate_jhu_radiologist_gpu.py:326-340 infers spacing from size ratios
   and replaces direction/origin when directions differ. A second fresh
   actual-evaluator test represented the same physical mask with opposite
   X direction and corresponding origin. Correct physical Dice was1.0;
   the evaluator returned0.0. Do not invent a source grid from GT extent.
   If prediction metadata is known corrupt, repair from producer provenance
   explicitly, rather than treating a different direction as corruption.

I4 [P1] Training/sampling/rendering evaluate different image patches.
   Dataset preprocessing uses MONAI center cropping at
   multiphase_paired_dataset.py:116-120. Sampling takes the low-index corner
   at sample_multiphase_diffusion.py:145-154. A20-cubed ramp cropped to8-cubed
   gave xHU[0,10,...70] in sampling versus[60,70,...130] in training/figures.
   The new figure generator:65-73 uses the centered crop, but
   evaluate_synthesis.py:71-80 still uses the corner. Share one preprocessing
   implementation and preserve its crop transform in outputs.

I5 [P1] Inference anatomy conditioning differs from training semantics.
   sample_multiphase_diffusion.py:171-187 discovers only subject.nii(.gz)
   and clamps labels to0..4. A full-body duodenum label19 becomes class4
   (colon), rather than GI class2. Missing suffix variants silently lead to
   a neutral background prior at193-196. Training now supports several
   filenames and explicit semantic remapping. Reuse that contract during
   inference; reject incompatible or missing conditioning when required.

I6 [P1] Stage2 launch defaults still reinterpret full-body organ labels.
   run_p10_stage2_synth.sbatch:68 selects results/totalseg_masks without
   --totalseg_task, while trainer:280 and dataset:65 default to'gi'.
   Current full-body labels1..4 are not the four GI targets, but dataset
   lines225-228 retain them as such. Supply and validate producer schema in
   the launcher and checkpoint/inference manifests, not only in unit tests.

I7 [P1] Pancreas-schema guessing can still accept current-version lung.
   generate_multiphase_tumors.py:104-107 prefers pancreas7, then accepts10
   if7 is absent and maximum label<=104. That condition also matches a
   current-total partial mask containing lung_upper_lobe_left10. Exact-function
   probes accept1000 voxels for both7 and10. The installed maps confirm
   pancreas7 in total and10 in total_v1; provenance must choose the schema.
   Binary host masks also require explicit organ identity.

I8 [P1] Showcase export assigns geometry without proving alignment.
   generate_showcase.py:411 discards NRRD geometry;421 wraps annotation
   voxels with the raw CT affine. Lines575-580 accept equal-sized model masks
   or shape-zoom them without a physical transform;680 stamps CT metadata
   onto the result. This may produce a well-formed yet misregistered NIfTI.
   Load GT metadata and resample both labels into actual CT physical space.

I9 [P1] CPU and GPU JHU routes remain incompatible.
   evaluate_jhu_radiologist_set.py:25-31 retains the old wrong bowel/colon map,
   includes esophagus, and105-107 compares raw GI prediction IDs without

I10 [P1] Minimum-coverage flags do not control pass decisions.
    Clinical evaluator235-241 and stress evaluator331-336 record>50-voxel

I11 [P2] Validation independence and patient counts are not established.
    train_stage2_synthesis.py:364-369 random-splits pair rows, ignoring

    Actual overlap requires the unavailable production manifest to quantify.
    compile_manuscript_tables.py:154 still counts distinct scan subject_id

I12 [P2] Inference output geometry cannot establish anatomical containment.
